About Silly Crow Studios

An introduction to the studio and what Silly Crow actually means

What is Silly Crow Studios?#

Simply put, it is a fever dream. A wild ambition of a lone person who is just trying to do fun things. I have quite limited experience in making things as one might tell from the state of this website. To fix that, I am trying to make things. To give myself a semblance of relevancy and progress, I created this Silly Crow brand so that I can attach a name to whatever I make.

Why not use your actual name, Justin?#

Cause it's boring. The crows are silly, absolutely nutty I say and that is fun. That is what life is all about, little moments of joy and beloning that make things seem like it is worth it.

So yes, I am Justin Clouthier a chemical engineer by study, and a whatever I need to be engineer by practice. With development, game making, the entirety of the outdoors as hobbies join me on this adventure as I discover what little moments I can find that bring joy. Silly Crow Studios is a small, very rough-around-the-edges game studio started by Justin Clouthier — a systems engineer who decided he wanted to make something that brings joy.

Right now that means working through the 20 Games Challenge one game at a time, building with Bevy and writing about it on the blog. Every finished game ends up on the projects page, where you can play it right in your browser.
